## 💬 How to Communicate with ALYSSA

### Text-Based Communication

Simply type your question or concern in the chat box and press Enter. Here are some examples:

**For Technical Issues:**
- "My internet isn't working"
- "The connection keeps dropping during video calls"
- "My WiFi is very slow today"
- "I can't connect to the internet"

**For Billing Questions:**
- "What's my current bill amount?"
- "When is my next payment due?"
- "I want to see my billing history"
- "Why is my bill higher this month?"

**For Service Plans:**
- "What internet plan do I currently have?"
- "I need faster internet for working from home"
- "What are my upgrade options?"
- "How much would it cost to upgrade my plan?"

**For Appointments:**
- "I need a technician to come fix my internet"
- "Can someone come check my connection?"
- "Schedule a service appointment"
- "What are your available appointment times?"

### Voice Communication (Optional)

1. **Click the microphone button** in the chat interface
2. **Speak clearly** and naturally
3. **Wait for ALYSSA to process** your speech
4. **Continue the conversation** as normal

## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

### Be Specific
**Instead of:** "My internet is bad"
**Try:** "My internet connection drops every few minutes during video calls"

### Provide Context
**Instead of:** "I have a problem"
**Try:** "I've been having slow internet speeds since yesterday, especially when multiple devices are connected"

### Ask Follow-up Questions
- "Can you explain that in more detail?"
- "What are my other options?"
- "How long will this take to resolve?"

### Confirm Understanding
- "So you're saying I need to upgrade my plan?"
- "Just to confirm, the technician will come tomorrow at 3 PM?"
